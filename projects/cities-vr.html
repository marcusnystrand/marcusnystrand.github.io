Cities: VR is a VR adaptation of Cities: Skylines developed by Fast Travel Games. I created various marketing materials, such as key art, logo, trailers and screenshots.

Here's the launch trailer for the PSVR2 version of the game. I recorded all gameplay in the VR headset. The in-game city was built by one of the developers.
I created the cinematic captions in Unreal Engine 5, using buildings and textures from the game.
I created and rendered the key art in Cinema 4D and did a paintover in Photoshop. For the logo I was required to use the original "Cities" part, so it was just a matter of designing the "VR" text.
Here's a wallpaper I created for the game release. The blue bird thing is the mascot of the game.
I also turned the wallpaper into a poster, included in gift boxes.
