Well Keeper is my first solo-developed and self-published game for Steam. It's a bullet hell roguelite where you move by flapping. Battle waves of enemies, upgrade abilities and become ridiculously powerful.

This game was my part-time hobby project from 2023 to 2026, while working at Might and Delight. I created all game and marketing assets myself, with a few exceptions. My partner illustrated the trees and icons for the skill tree. She also illustrated the key art. Two friends of mine did the CJK localization. No AI was used.
